GENERIC WEAPONRY

Generic Weaponry is a catch-all term for a wide variety of small, low-threat units. They are generally smaller than Muscle Tracers, and pose even less of a threat to Armored Cores, but are easily mass produced.

CV-T-020

Half-track Combat Vehicle | Belius Applied Weapon Systems

A compact, armored half-track designed for frontline support and rapid deployment of weapons systems. While obsolete and almost completely ineffective aginst ACs, they still serve as both a light APC and a weapons platform.

CH-T-025

Combat Helicopter | Belius Applied Weapon Systems

A combat helicopter that uses its nose gun and a heavy weapon attached below the fuselage to attack its targets. Although equipped with a gigantic weapon for its size, it struggles to damage ACs except at extremely close ranges, where they rarely survive for long.

TH-E-012

Transport Helicopter | Belius Applied Weapon Systems

A large quad rotor helicopter that serves as the main form of aerial transport for the RLF. Its 420 load capacity allows it to carry multiple MTs, bulk loads of resources, or even an entire AC hangar for mobile repair and resupply.

MUSCLE TRACERS

Also known simply as MTs, Muscle Tracers refer to a versatile classification of mech that, as their name entails, ‘traces’ the movements of the operator and translates them to the limbs of the MT. This makes MTs very intuitive and easy to use, but limited in speed and agility in comparison to ACs.

MT-T-026

Guard MT | Belius Applied Weapon Systems

A small, reverse-jointed, chicken walker style mech that moves around quite slowly, has relatively weak armaments, and can be destroyed by almost anything intended for anti-armor. These craft are most prized for their ability to be mass produced, and often fill the role of low security guard details or anti infantry units.

MT-E-104

Light MT | Belius Applied Weapon Systems

A boxy, manned, bipedal mech with thrusters on its back that give it a limited ability to skate short distances, as well as jump up to rooftops to gain a better vantage point. They can be taken down by a few well placed shots from any anti-tank weapon, but their strength lies in numbers. It's mass-produced nature means they are often fought in packs or squads, and are never alone.

MT-J-048

Transport Helicopter | Belius Applied Weapon Systems

A high performance model when compared to their bipedal mass produced counterparts, the BAWS Tetrapod MT is a notable force to be reckoned with, capable of tearing up scores of MTs on its own using a wide variety of both standard and unique armaments. The BAWS Tetrapod MT is a large, boxy, manned quadruped mech with large thrusters on its legs and underside, allowing it to jump, slide and dash good distances, making it a highly mobile threat. Compared to the box-shaped standard BAWS MT, the Tetrapod is twice as large and features more effective armor, allowing it to sustain large amounts of punishment.
